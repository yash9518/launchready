LaunchReady — how one check runs

Is this packaging artwork ready to print? Every finding is tied to a rule quote and a box on the artwork.

Inputs

ArtworkPrint-ready PDF (DeskPack / Illustrator export), flattened PNG, or a phone photo of a proof
RulebookCompany rule cards (Markdown) or free text: regulation extracts, PDF, Word
Approved dataContent sheet / product spec: Excel, CSV, JSON, PDF, Word
→

1 · Read

VECTOR + VISION
  • PDF text layer: exact words, fonts, sizes, positions (PyMuPDF)
  • Paint-order test finds text hidden under artwork
  • Raster: deskew, neural OCR (PP-OCR via RapidOCR / ONNX), second pass on uncertain words
  • Dieline → panels, front panel found; barcodes (zxing-cpp); veg / symbols (OpenCV); glyph heights in mm

2 · Understand

FACTS WITH SOURCES
  • Statement of identity, net quantity, ingredients, allergens, nutrition panel (US or India), claims, address, barcode…
  • Every fact keeps the IDs of the text lines it came from
  • Content sheet normalised into approved fields; its own gaps and contradictions are reported

3 · Rules

RULE CARDS
  • Each rule = quote + source + one of 24 check types + severity + why it matters
  • Free-text rulebooks compiled into cards (LLM, or offline pattern compiler)
  • Effective dates: in force, upcoming, proposed
  • User can switch rules off and re-run instantly

4 · Check

DETERMINISTIC CODE
  • Presence and placement (front panel)
  • Text = approved copy, word diff
  • Nutrition arithmetic, %DV, physical plausibility
  • Claims vs nutrients and ingredients (vegan + honey)
  • Allergen coverage, barcode GS1, text height, cross-panel consistency

5 · Verify & decide

AVOID FALSE ALARMS
FAILcode proved it on a confident read
REVIEWuncertain read, judgment, or AI-only
PASSwith the evidence that satisfied it
COMINGrule not yet in force on the date
  • "Missing" is re-checked before it is reported
→

Outputs

Review screenVerdict, findings by severity, evidence boxes on the artwork, click to zoom, rule quote, expected vs found
Ask about a findingFollow-up questions answered from that finding's evidence
ReportPrintable HTML/PDF with evidence crops, plus JSON
Optional AI layerGemini / Vertex AI, Claude or GPT. Every AI output is a proposal that code verifies. An AI alone can never mark a rule as failed.
Compile rulesTurns free-text regulations into rule cards. Each quote must exist in the source or the card is dropped.
Second readerReads photos alongside OCR. Must cite OCR line IDs; disagreement sends the item to review.
Judgment rules"Does the imagery mislead?" Can pass or flag for review, never fail.
Confirm "missing"Second look before an absence is reported on raster artwork.
Answer questionsGrounded only in the finding, its rule quote and its evidence lines.
